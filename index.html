In Between

Residential – Commercial Building

The main concern behind this project was the lack of social spaces and connection with nature in residential buildings. The primary design goal was to connect the three building volumes and create public spaces both at ground level and across the upper floors.

Rasta Sport Complex

Sport and Mental Wellness Center

The Sport and Mental Wellness Center approaches architecture as an essential part of the human well-being experience. Spatial proportions, natural light, landscape, color, and the connection between indoor and outdoor environments are carefully considered to create spaces that support activity, comfort, concentration, and relaxation.

Children Hospital

Healthcare

Inspired by LEGO blocks, the project explores modular forms, varied volumes, and vibrant colors to create a playful, child-friendly architectural identity. The concept aims to transform the hospital from a purely clinical environment into a welcoming and engaging space that supports children’s emotional well-being and recovery.

Iran Pavilion

Exhibition Pavilion — Dubai Expo

The Iran Pavilion at Dubai Expo is inspired by the natural landscape of Kenarak in Sistan and Baluchestan. The project combines traditional Iranian architecture, earth-based construction, and cultural heritage to create an immersive experience.
