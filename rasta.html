Sport and Mental Wellness Center

Concept

The Sport and Mental Wellness Center approaches architecture as an essential part of the human well-being experience. Spatial proportions, natural light, landscape, color, and the connection between indoor and outdoor environments are carefully considered to create spaces that support activity, comfort, concentration, and relaxation.

Design Process

Based on the site shape and the concept of combining architecture with nature, a central Green Spine connects all departments. The building masses are arranged along this path, while a loop of walking and cycling paths surrounds the site. Outdoor sports courts are placed according to each department's needs, providing indoor, semi-open, and outdoor spaces with direct access to nature.

Zoning and Spatial Organization

The site is organized into specialized sports and wellness departments, each positioned according to its functional requirements and spatial relationships. The Green Spine acts as the main connecting element, providing a clear circulation route between departments while integrating landscape into the overall design. A peripheral walking and cycling path encourages active movement throughout the site, while outdoor sports facilities and shared amenities enhance accessibility, interaction, and the connection between architecture and nature.
